Jotv has a refreshed look: rounded pills with a filled accent focus, a slim side menu or a Netflix-style top menu with Search, and lists that start under the menu with the next row peeking in. Search sits beside View and Sort and searches the category in use. Picture avatars show whole, settings are reordered by use, and the phone remote is easier to reach one-handed.
