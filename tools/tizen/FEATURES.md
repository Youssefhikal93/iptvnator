# Jo-TV features

Jo-TV brings live channels, movies and shows from your saved sources to your Samsung TV. Use the arrows to move, OK to choose and Return to go back. The names below are the English names shown on the TV.

Kept up to date with every ticket; see the rule in docs/CONTEXT.md.

## What's new

- Full backup with a computer: Settings → Backup → Send everything to computer… and Restore from computer… keep every profile, setting and your library (see Backup below).
- Samsung home screen tiles: press Home on the remote and move to the Jo-TV icon to see Continue watching and recent channels; pick one to play it straight away (see Home and your library below).
- Converted sound appears only when it can help, and tells you whether the provider's HLS or TS sound now plays or no other sound was found.
- Multi-view: watch 2, 3 or 4 live channels at once. Press Blue while watching, or hold OK → Multi-view (see Live TV below).
- The live player's menu is now called Options, and every action in it and in the hold-OK menus has an icon.
- Arabic: the player menu is fully translated and reads right to left, category headings are translated, and channel numbers stay next to their names.
- The channel list remembers keys pressed while its channels are still loading.
- Sync subtitles: a bar over the playing video. Press OK when you hear the line it shows, then fine-tune with ◀ ▶ (0.1 s) and ▲ ▼ (1 s); Hold OK to pick the line you just heard from the list instead. The next episode of the same release starts with the same timing.
- Find subtitles online shows each result's full name first (up to two lines), the video format under it (1080p · WEB-DL …), then the site, ★ and download badges, so a subtitle for another show with the same name is easy to spot.

## Live TV

### Now and next (#1)

Live TV lists show the current programme when guide information is available. Press OK while watching full screen to see Now and Next; Settings → Playback → Programme guide (now and next) turns this information on or off.

### Channel list and channel banner (#8, #79)

While watching a live channel, press ◀ to open the channel list, even when the player controls are showing. Use ▲/▼ to choose a channel, ◀/▶ to change category and OK to watch; Return closes the list. Outside the list, ▲/▼ or CH + / CH - change channel and show a short banner with its name and programme.

### Last channel and previous channel (#8)

Settings → Playback → On start open → Last channel opens your saved channel when the app starts; choose Home to start there instead. While watching live TV, PRE-CH or Green returns to the previous channel. Without those keys, hold OK and choose Previous channel followed by its name.

### Live preview (#32)

In the Live TV List view, press OK on a channel to play it beside the list, then OK on that same channel for full screen. Moving the focus does not change the playing channel; OK on another channel changes it. Settings → Playback → Live preview → Off opens channels full screen straight away; Grid also opens full screen.

### Recent channels (#80)

Live TV → Recent channels lists channels you watched for at least 10 seconds, newest first. It also appears among the categories in the channel list while watching. To empty it, hold OK on a channel inside Recent channels and choose Clear recent channels.

### Your own live groups and channel order (#76)

Hold OK on a live channel → Add to group, then choose a group or New group. Hold OK on an own group's category to Rename group or Delete group, or on one of its channels to Remove from group. In an own group or live Favorites, hold OK on a channel → Move, use ▲/▼ and press OK to place it or Return to cancel; changing channels follows your saved order.

### Multi-view (#77)

While watching a live channel, press Blue, or hold OK → Multi-view, and choose 2 tiles, 3 tiles or 4 tiles; the channel you were watching becomes tile 1. Use the arrows to move between tiles: only the selected tile has sound, and CH + / CH - change its channel. OK on an empty tile (+ Add channel) opens the channel list; OK on a playing tile offers Full screen, Change channel, Make main and Remove. Hold OK for Layout, Audio and subtitles of the selected tile, or Leave multi-view; Return leaves and plays the selected channel full screen. Hold OK → Resume multi-view brings back your last layout and channels. If the provider refuses one channel, only that tile shows Retry.

### Reconnect after a live interruption (#14)

If live TV stops, Jo-TV shows Reconnecting… and tries again after closing the old connection. If it cannot reconnect, choose Retry playback with the arrows and OK, or Back to leave the player.

## TV guide

### Full programme grid (#6)

In Live TV, press Guide or move to the TV guide button above the list and press OK. Use ▲/▼ for channels, ◀/▶ for programmes and CH + / CH - for pages; OK on a programme on now watches its channel, while OK on a past or future programme opens its details. Return goes back to Live TV.

### Your own guide links (#7)

Open Settings → Sources, press OK on a source → Guide link…, and fill in Guide link 1 (optional), Guide link 2 (optional) or Guide link 3 (optional). Use XMLTV links (.xml or .xml.gz), adjust Guide time offset (hours, -12 to +12) if needed, then choose Save. The source's menu also offers Refresh guide and Remove guide links once links are saved.

### Guide order and automatic matching

For an Xtream source, the guide form's Provider's guide choice puts it Before my guides or After my guides. For finer control, open the source's Guide order menu, choose a guide, then Move up or Move down; each channel uses the first guide with programmes and falls through empty ones. Automatic matching recognises SP as SPORTS, keeps MAX and XTRA distinct, and keeps 4K when it names a channel rather than picture quality.

### Choose a channel's guide by hand

With guide links saved, hold OK on a channel in Live TV and choose Guide channel: Automatic, or the saved guide channel shown there. Choose a suggested name, or Show all guide channels followed by the count to browse names; very large guides show only the first 300 names there. Choose Automatic to return to automatic matching.

### Past programmes and catch-up (#19)

When an Xtream source offers catch-up for a channel, the guide lets you move left to its available past programmes. Press OK to open a programme's details, then OK on Watch from start to play it. Choose Back to live in the player controls to return to the live channel.

### Start the current programme again (#82)

On a live channel with catch-up and a current programme, hold OK while watching → Start over. Use Back to live in the controls to return; Jo-TV also offers it when you catch up with live TV. The option is absent when the source cannot replay that programme.

### Programme reminders (#75)

In the guide, choose a future programme with OK, then press OK on Remind me; the same place offers Remove reminder afterwards. Jo-TV shows a reminder one minute before the start, with Watch now and Dismiss, only while the app is open. Press Yellow in the guide, or ▲ from its first channel and choose Reminders, to see and remove saved reminders; Settings → Playback → Reminders opens the same list.

### Search for a programme (#81)

In the guide, press Blue, or ▲ from the first channel and OK on Search programmes. Enter a name to search programmes already saved on the TV, grouped as On now, Later today, Coming days and Past (catch-up). Use the arrows and OK to watch a channel, play available catch-up or set a future reminder; reopen the search to include programmes loaded since it opened.

## Movies and Series

### Movie details (#3)

In Movies, select a film and press OK for its plot, artwork, facts and available actions. Choose Play, Resume from followed by a time, Start over or Add to Favorites with ◀/▶ and OK; ▼ reaches More in followed by the category name. Holding OK on a film in the list opens options with Details first, then Play and the other available actions.

### Shows, seasons and episodes (#4)

In Series, press OK on a show to open its details, seasons and episodes. Press ▼ to reach the episodes, ▲ from an episode to reach the season buttons, and ◀/▶ to choose another season or episode; OK on an episode plays it. The main button offers the next unwatched episode or one you can continue, and each season shows its watched count.

### Movies and shows in M3U playlists (#16)

An M3U source also offers Movies and Series when its playlist contains recognisable films or episodes. Open either section and use OK on a title just as with other sources; episode names such as S01E02 let Jo-TV group a show into seasons. Details depend on what the playlist supplies, with extra information available when TMDB is set up.

### Recently added and sorting (#9)

For Xtream Movies and Series, Recently added shows up to 50 of the newest dated additions to the source, rather than the newest release years. To change the order of a normal category or this source's Favorites, press ▲ at the top of the list, choose Sort and select Newest, Oldest, Name A-Z, Name Z-A, Top rated or Provider order. Recently added stays newest first, and Recently watched and Continue watching keep their own order.

### Mark watched or unwatched (#12)

Hold OK on a film → Mark as watched, or on a show → Mark show as watched. On a show's detail page, hold OK on an episode for Mark episode watched or Mark season watched. The same menus offer the matching unwatched actions, and items played to the end are marked watched automatically.

### New episodes (#13)

A favorite or recently watched show can show New episodes when the source reports additions since you last watched or opened it. Open Home → New episodes or choose the badged show in Series and press OK. Opening its detail page clears the badge until another addition is found.

### Hide watched titles (#59)

In Movies or Series, press ▲ at the top of the list, move to Hide watched and press OK to hide watched films and fully watched shows. Use the same button again, or Show watched titles on an all-watched empty list, to bring them back. Search still finds watched titles, and Home keeps showing them.

### Extra information and actor pages (#20)

Save your own TMDB API key in Settings → TMDB → TMDB API key to add available artwork, facts, cast and Similar in your library to detail pages. On a detail page, move down to Cast, select a person and press OK for their biography and titles marked In your library. Choose a title there with OK to open it; only titles found in the current source are offered.

### Find titles by genre (#20)

In Movies or Series, press ▲ at the top of the list, move to the Genre button beside Search and choose a genre with OK. It narrows the open list using the source's genre information and, with a TMDB key, checks more titles in batches of 20. Choose Check 20 more when offered to continue, or All to clear the filter.

### Trailers in YouTube (#38)

When a detail page offers Trailer, select it with ◀/▶ and press OK to open the TV's YouTube app. Return in YouTube brings you back to the detail page. The button depends on an available trailer and YouTube support on the TV.

### Movie collections and Refresh (#58, #67)

A film's detail page can show its collection: move down to the row and use ◀/▶ and OK to open another available film; missing films say Not available. Movies → Collections groups related films by their names, or with richer information when TMDB is on. In Collections, press ▲ from the top row to reach Refresh and press OK to check the films again in the background while keeping the known collections visible.

## Home and your library

### Home rows (#5)

Choose Home in the main menu, then use the arrows and OK to open or play titles in its rows. Rows include Continue watching, favorites, recently watched channels and recently added titles when there is something to show. Settings → Display → Home layout offers Cinema, Big first row or Backdrop.

### Continue watching (#31, #33)

Home → Continue watching brings back unfinished films and episodes with their saved progress; select one and press OK to continue. The Movies and Series categories also offer Continue watching. To remove a saved position, hold OK on its item and choose Remove from Continue watching.

### Tiles on the Samsung home screen (#91)

Press Home on the remote and move to the Jo-TV icon: up to six Continue watching titles and six recently watched channels from the active source show as tiles. Pick one to open Jo-TV and play it; a film or episode continues where it stopped. The tiles change when you leave Jo-TV, at most once every 10 minutes (a Samsung limit). Titles from a locked category never show. Turn them off in Settings → Playback → Samsung home screen tiles. Setup and removal: HOME-TILES.md.

### Favorites and recents across sources (#24)

Hold OK on a channel, film or show → Add to Favorites, then find it in Favorites or the matching Home row; the same menu offers Remove from Favorites. Played items appear in Recently watched, where their options can Remove from Recently watched. Settings → Display → Favorites and recents from → All sources combines your library across saved sources; This source limits it to the source in use.

### Search titles and the open category (#31, #69)

On Home, press ▲ to the Search icon, then OK and enter a name to search Movies, Series and Live TV in the current source. In a section, press ▲ at the top of the list and OK on Search to search the category in use. Choose All channels, All movies or All series first to search that whole section.

### Because you watched (#63, #72)

With TMDB set up, Home can offer Because you watched followed by a title, using films and shows you played for at least five minutes or marked watched. It offers similar titles available in the current source when enough matches are found. Use the arrows and OK on a suggestion to open it.

## Player

### Pause, timeline and playback speed (#31)

For a film or episode, OK or Play / Pause pauses or plays; ▼ shows hidden controls, and ◀/▶ move along their buttons. Press ▲ from those buttons to select the timeline, then ◀/▶ to move through the video and OK to confirm the jump. Select Audio & subtitles or the Settings gear with OK for their menus; Yellow / Info opens the full player menu, where Playback speed offers the speeds supported by the player.

### Previous, Next and Up next (#31, #70)

While watching an episode, show the controls and use ◀/▶ and OK on Previous or Next to change episode, or on Episodes to choose with ◀/▶ for seasons and ▲/▼ for episodes. Up next appears near the end: OK plays now and Return hides or cancels it. Settings → Playback → Autoplay next episode offers Off, 5 s, 8 s or 15 s for the end-of-episode countdown; Jo-TV also reuses known playback choices to shorten later starts.

### Learn and skip a show's intro (#31)

On the timeline, skip forward at least 20 seconds within an episode's first five minutes to teach Jo-TV that show's intro. In later episodes, press ▲ from the timeline to select Skip intro when it appears, then OK to jump past it. To teach it again, open the player menu → Intro → Forget this show’s intro.

### Confirmed resume position (#33)

Choose Resume from followed by the saved time on a film's detail page, or continue an episode, to return to your progress. Jo-TV checks that playback reached the saved point before replacing that progress. If resume fails, choose Retry with OK or move to Start over to begin again.

### Audio tracks, embedded subtitles and converted sound (#29, #54, #55, #92)

While watching, press Yellow / Info for the menu, then use ▲/▼ and OK on Audio or Subtitles to choose an available track; Off hides subtitles. The TV's Samsung player handles films and episodes, and the audio list can also show tracks labelled Not supported on this TV. For Xtream films and episodes with suitable provider links, Audio offers Converted sound (provider) only when all the file's sound tracks are unsupported or no sound was found. While converted sound plays, both that checked row and The file's own sound stay available so you can switch back. A manual try, or the automatic try after five seconds without sound, tells you whether the provider's converted sound now plays (HLS or TS) or no other sound was found. If neither address has sound, playback continues on the original file where you were.

### Play links that refuse the first check

Choose Play on a film's detail page, or select an episode and press OK. Some providers block Jo-TV's first check of a link even though it can play; the TV can now try that link directly.

### Download subtitles (#30, #52)

In Settings → Subtitles, add your own OpenSubtitles API key or SubDL API key under Subtitles download and choose Subtitle language. During a film or episode, press Yellow / Info → Subtitles → Find subtitles online…, then choose a result with the arrows and OK. Jo-TV searches both services when their keys are set, remembers the chosen download, and offers Try another match when there are more results.

### Backup subtitle search and translation

Settings → Subtitles → Backup search allows extra searches only when OpenSubtitles and SubDL find nothing. Add a Subsource API key for its results or a DeepL API key for translation, then use Find subtitles online… in the player's Subtitles menu. Translation is offered as Translate from another language when available; it starts only when you choose it.

### Align downloaded subtitles with the voice (#39)

With downloaded subtitles showing, open the player menu → Subtitle timing → Sync subtitles. A bar at the top shows the next line; the video keeps playing, and when you hear that line you press OK. Then ◀ ▶ move the subtitles by 0.1 s and ▲ ▼ by 1 s while you watch; OK keeps the timing and Return puts it back. Hold OK in the bar, or choose Subtitle timing → Choose the line…, to pick the line you just heard with ▲ ▼ (CH + / CH - for a page): OK moves the subtitles so it starts where the video was, and the video carries on. Reset timing removes the delay. The timing is remembered for that title, and the next episode of the same release starts with it.

### Subtitle style and picture shape (#26)

Open the player menu → Subtitle style to change subtitle size, colour, background or position; Settings → Subtitles keeps the same style choices. Player menu → Picture → Aspect offers Auto, Fit, Fill, 16:9 or 4:3, also available in Settings → Playback → Aspect. Live preview always uses Fit.

### Return hides controls before leaving (#37)

While controls are visible, Return hides them first; another Return leaves full-screen playback. An open menu or panel closes before the player, and live playback opened from preview returns to that preview. Return from the preview stops it.

### Stream information (#84)

Open the player menu → Stream info → Show on screen to see available picture, sound and connection information while watching. Values the player cannot report show a dash. Return hides this overlay before hiding controls or leaving playback.

## Sources and saved data

### Add, switch and manage sources (#15, #51)

Open Settings → Sources → + Add Xtream account or + Add M3U playlist, enter the details with the remote and choose Save. OK on a saved source opens Watch this source or Switch to this source, Refresh source, Edit source… and Remove; the source in use has an Active badge. Xtream sources also show account status, expiry and connection details when the provider supplies them, with a warning near expiry.

### Add a source from your phone (#85)

In an add-source form, choose Use my phone with the remote, then scan the QR code or open its displayed link on a phone on the same home network. Enter the source on the phone and choose Send to TV; check the received details on the TV and press OK on Save. The code works once for 10 minutes, and New code gives you another if it expires.

### Start with the saved catalogue (#2)

After a source has loaded once, Jo-TV uses its saved catalogue on later starts so you can browse without waiting for fresh lists. Choose a source and open Home, Live TV, Movies or Series as usual; open a film or show to see saved details while older details refresh. For new lists now, use Settings → Sources → Refresh content now; scheduled background updates follow Refresh automatically.

### Automatic source and guide refresh (#86)

Settings → Sources → Refresh automatically offers Daily at night, Every 12 hours or Off. Refresh runs while Jo-TV is open, idle and nothing is playing, including no live preview; Settings → Sources shows Last refreshed for each source. A rejected login or failed download keeps the previous catalogue, and new lists are fetched in full before they replace the old ones.

### Library stays with the source account (#34)

Use Settings → Sources, OK on a source → Edit source… to change its details. Changing the Xtream server or username, or an M3U playlist link, keeps the old account's favorites, recents and progress apart from the new account's library. Changing only the source name or Xtream password keeps the same library.

### Loading, empty lists and errors (#17)

Jo-TV shows loading messages and explains empty lists, such as No favorites yet or Nothing to continue. On a source or network error, use the arrows and OK on Retry; an account error also offers Go to Sources to fix the login. A Too many requests message waits and retries by itself.

## Profiles and parental lock

### Separate profiles and avatars (#23, #43)

Select your avatar beside the main menu and press OK to switch profile or choose Manage profiles…. In Settings → Profiles, use Add profile… for up to six viewers, Avatar to change the picture, Rename profile… or Delete profile; each viewer keeps their own library and settings while sources stay shared. Ask who’s watching on start controls whether the profile picker appears at start when there is more than one profile; hold OK on a profile in that picker to change its name or avatar.

### Parental PIN lock (#21)

In Settings → Parental lock → PIN, choose Set PIN… and enter and confirm four digits, using number keys or arrows and OK. Hold OK on a category → Lock category; OK on the locked category asks for the PIN before its items become available. Re-lock after controls how long it stays unlocked, and the PIN row also lets you change or remove the PIN.

### Kids profiles (#62)

From a grown-up profile, open Settings → Profiles → Add profile…, enter a name, turn on Kids profile and choose Next: choose categories. Set the guardian's PIN if asked, tick the allowed categories for each source with OK and choose Save; later use Settings → Profiles → Kids profiles to change them. Only ticked categories appear, new categories stay hidden, Settings is unavailable, and leaving the Kids profile needs the guardian's PIN.

### Preferred and remembered sound and subtitle languages (#60, #83)

Settings → Playback → Preferred sound and Preferred subtitles set this profile's first choices for available tracks. Show subtitles offers Always or Only when I don't understand the sound. A manual Audio or Subtitles choice in the player is remembered for that channel or show, including Off, and takes priority over the profile preference when that track is available; online subtitle downloads remain a manual choice.

## Look, navigation and comfort

### Settings and app details (#10, #65)

Choose Settings in the main menu, use ▲/▼ to choose a group and OK or ▶ to open its rows. Press OK on a row to switch it, open its choices or run its action; ◀ or Return goes back to the groups. Settings → About shows the Jo-TV app name and App version; look for the Jo-TV name and icon when launching it on the TV.

### Main menu at the side or top (#50)

Settings → Display → Menu position offers Side or Top. In the side menu use ▲/▼ to choose a section and ▶ to enter; in the top menu use ◀/▶ and ▼. Return from a browsing list goes back to the main menu.

### List, Grid, Wide boxes and Info boxes (#66, #69, #73)

Press ▲ at the top of a category or item list, move to View and press OK to choose the layout, even if Settings → Display → Top bar is Off. Live TV offers List and Grid; Movies and Series also offer Wide boxes and Info boxes, with film length or season counts when known. Open a title once to remember those facts, or set up TMDB to fill them in while browsing; Settings → Display also lets you choose the default view for each section.

### Hide categories (#11)

Hold OK on a source category → Hide category or Hide all except this. To bring categories back, open Settings → Display → Manage Live TV categories, Manage Movies categories or Manage Series categories and use OK to show or hide each one; Show all restores them. Hiding is saved for this profile and source, without asking for a PIN.

### Change category order (#87)

Hold OK on a category → Move, use ▲/▼ to move it, then OK to place it or Return to cancel. The same menu offers Move to top and Reset order, and the category-management screens support these actions too. Your order is remembered for the profile and source; new source categories are added after the saved ones.

### Themes, colour and spacing (#27, #64)

In Settings → Display, Theme offers Dark black, Dark grey, Dark blue and Light. Accent colour changes the focus colour, and Density offers Comfortable or Compact. Set Animations to Off if movement is uncomfortable or makes the TV stutter; these choices belong to the current profile.

### App language (#27)

Open Settings → Display → Language and choose with the arrows and OK. English, العربية (Arabic) and other available languages change menus and help text; Arabic also changes the reading direction. Source title names are supplied by the source.

### Screen savers (#61, #71, #74)

Settings → Display → Screen saver chooses Off, 5 min, 10 min or 15 min before the app's saver appears on idle menus, paused video or live preview. Screen saver to use offers This app's for title pictures, Logo and clock for a centred scene with new sizes and movements every two minutes, or The TV's own for Samsung's saver. Press any key to dismiss the app's saver; that press does not also select or play anything.

### Sleep timer (#22)

While watching, open the player menu → Sleep timer and choose 15 min, 30 min, 60 min or 90 min, or End of movie / End of episode when offered. It stops playback and closes Jo-TV at the chosen time; choose Off to cancel. The final warning offers Keep watching and Stop now with the arrows and OK.

### Help and actions without coloured keys (#89)

In a list, press ▲ at the top and choose the ? Help button; Home has ? beside Search, the guide has Help above its first channel, Settings has About → Help, and the player menu has Help. Without coloured keys, hold OK on live TV for the menu and Previous channel, or use ▲ from the guide's first channel to reach Search programmes and Reminders; in Home Search, press ▼ after typing, then ▶ and OK for Search programmes. Help shows the keys for that screen, including the hint Coloured buttons: press 123 on your remote, and a small ring fills while you hold OK to open options.

## Backup and desktop transfer

### Import a desktop backup (#25)

Open Settings → Backup → Import from link…, enter a direct HTTP or HTTPS link to an IPTVnator desktop backup and choose Download. Check the counts, then use the arrows and OK on Merge to keep existing sources and add missing items, or Replace to overwrite sources and the library; Cancel leaves them as they are. This manually transfers supported Xtream accounts and M3U playlist links, favorites, recents and available progress.

### Export a backup (#25)

Open Settings → Backup → Export, read Contains your passwords and choose Continue. Download JSON on this device saves the backup where supported; Copy to a link… lets you send it to a trusted server with Upload with PUT or Upload with POST and shows a phone link only after checking it can read the backup back. Export uses the current profile's library across sources; desktop transfer does not preserve every TV choice, and M3U progress does not survive a transfer through the desktop.

### Everything to and from a computer

Settings → Backup → Send everything to computer… copies every profile, PIN, setting, source and your whole library to a computer on the home network; Restore from computer… puts it all back exactly as it was, to reinstall Jo-TV or move to a new TV. The computer runs a small receiver that shows the address and a code to enter; steps in JOTV-BACKUP.md.
